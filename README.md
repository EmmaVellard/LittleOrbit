# Little Orbit

![Little Orbit — My year among the stars](docs/images/little-orbit-banner.jpg)

### [Open Little Orbit](https://emmavellard.github.io/LittleOrbit/)

Little Orbit is a gentle daily journal for keeping one small memory at a time. A sentence is enough: something that made you smile, a quiet detail, a place, a person, or a moment you do not want to lose.

As the days pass, your year becomes a constellation of softly colored dots — a calm visual record of the life you actually lived.

Every dot represents a day. Open today’s dot to write a memory, return to an earlier day, or switch between the full year and a closer monthly view. Filled days keep their own color, so the calendar slowly becomes unmistakably yours.

## Keep the moments that matter close

- Write or edit one memory for any past or present day.
- Find a moment again with search.
- Mark meaningful memories as favorites with the moon.
- Revisit memories from the same date in previous years.
- Let unfinished thoughts wait safely as local drafts.
- Download the whole journal as a readable Markdown keepsake.

![An open journal surrounded by moons, planets, and stars](docs/images/memory-journal.jpg)

## Your journal stays yours

Little Orbit keeps memories inside the browser on the device where they were written. Publishing or sharing the application does not publish anyone’s journal: every person and every device begins with a separate, empty orbit.

There is no automatic cloud journal or cross-device sync. To move memories between a computer and a phone, download a Markdown backup on one device and restore it on the other. Keep that backup somewhere private, as it contains the journal in readable text.

## Make it feel like an app

Little Orbit can be added to a phone’s Home Screen and opened without the usual browser frame.

- **iPhone:** open Little Orbit in Safari, tap **Share**, choose **Add to Home Screen**, enable **Open as Web App**, then tap **Add**.
- **Android:** open Little Orbit in Chrome, open the menu, then choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

Once it has been opened online, the journal is designed to remain available offline for quiet moments away from a connection.
